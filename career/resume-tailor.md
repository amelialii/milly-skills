# Resume Tailor

Inputs: my resume and the job description. Ask for whichever is missing.
Follow resume-bullet-rules.md for every bullet you write.

## 1. Analyze

Act as a senior recruiter for this exact company. Analyze my resume against this JD and give me:
- a match score out of 100
- the top 5 missing keywords
- the 3 red flags a hiring manager would spot in under 10 seconds

## 2. Rewrite

Rewrite my experience section and naturally include those keywords and remove the red flags. Use the Google XYZ formula: accomplished X as measured by Y by doing Z.

## 3. Skim test

Act as an ATS filter and a hiring manager reading 200 resumes in 1 sitting. Scan my new resume and tell me which section will get skipped, and rewrite them so they actually stop the scroll.
