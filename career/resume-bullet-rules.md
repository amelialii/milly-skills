# Resume Bullet Rules

- No company-internal or product-specific terms a recruiter without that company's context won't understand (e.g. "canvas," "job" in the watsonx sense). Replace with plain-English function that describes what the thing actually does.
- Don't use jargon that's just a fancier synonym for something already said (e.g. "harness" when you've already said "framework").
- No em-dashes.
- No excessive parentheses, minimal.
- Every bullet lands in either 0.5–1 line or 1.5–2 lines (Overleaf with LaTeX). Never between 1–1.5 lines to avoid wasted whitespace.
- Open every bullet with a hard build verb (Built, Created, etc.)
- Use the Google XYZ formula: accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z.
- Explicitly try to surface keywords that map to stated JD qualifications when applicable, especially minimum quals.
- Never invent numbers. Use brackets until a real figure is pulled and defended.
- Any claim must survive an interviewer's "percent of what / how'd you measure that" probe.
